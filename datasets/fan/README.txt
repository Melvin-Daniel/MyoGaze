Put fan training photos here (same idea as datasets/lamp/raw):

  datasets/fan/raw/fan_001.jpg
  datasets/fan/raw/fan_002.jpg
  ...

After labeling, each .jpg gets a matching .txt in this folder.

Label in the browser (server must be running):
  http://127.0.0.1:8000/label/fan
  (same as http://127.0.0.1:8000/label?dataset=fan)
